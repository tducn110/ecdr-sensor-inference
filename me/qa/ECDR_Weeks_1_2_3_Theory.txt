1     Executive brief

This Week 1 document fixes the research question, reference contract, evidence bound-
aries, and experimental plan for Event-Conditioned Certified Decision Reuse (ECDR) on the
HAPT smartphone inertial-sensing data. The primary reference is a new, frozen twelve-
class SmallCNN1D specification. The historical six-class checkpoint and the earlier valid-
convolution/GAP theory remain provenance only. The completed feasibility baseline is re-
ported below; it does not establish ECDR certification or net savings.


1.1   Problem and decision contract

Let Xt ∈ R6×128 be a raw sensor window and let P be fixed per-channel z-score preprocess-
ing, fitted on training subjects only. The reference function is F = fθ ◦ P , with logits F (X) ∈
R12 and deterministic tie-breaking. The reference decision is h(X) = arg maxk Fk (X). An
ECDR policy may reuse a previous decision only when its certificate proves that the current
input belongs to a region on which the same fixed reference decision holds. The primary
safety target is therefore ĥECDR (X) = h(X) with zero disagreements over the declared eval-
uation scope. Agreement with the reference is distinct from agreement with ground-truth
labels.
The main research question is: for the frozen SmallCNN1D K=12 reference on HAPT, can an
event-conditioned certificate preserve every reference decision while reducing total end-to-
end inference cost under a declared streaming workload? Cost includes reference forward
evaluation, preprocessing, event detection, membership tests, certificate construction or
reuse, fallback evaluation, and I/O or scheduling overhead. Forward-only timing cannot
establish a net saving.

The source is the UCI Smartphone-Based Recognition of Human Activities and Postural
Transitions dataset [1], sampled at 50 Hz with three accelerometer and three gyroscope
channels. Windows are 128 samples with stride 64; each window is tied to its recording and

1.3   Frozen reference architecture

The K=12 contract is: input (B, 6, 128); same-padded Conv1D 6→32, kernel 5, padding 2, fol-
lowed by BatchNorm, ReLU and MaxPool2; same-padded Conv1D 32→64, kernel 3, padding
1, followed by BatchNorm, ReLU and MaxPool2; same-padded Conv1D 64→64, kernel 3,
padding 1, followed by BatchNorm and ReLU; Flatten of 2,048 features; Linear 2,048→128,
ReLU; and Linear 128→12. The executed trainable parameter count is 283,692; shape and
parameter checks passed on the instantiated K=12 model. BatchNorm evaluation uses
frozen running statistics; a checkpoint and its preprocessing parameters are an inseparable
reference identity.
The operational checkpoint was selected after the completed training and evaluation run. It
uses seeds 42, 43, and 44; at most 30 epochs; Adam with learning rate 10−3 and weight decay
10−4 ; cosine scheduling; and patience 10. Epoch and seed selection use validation macro-
F1. Test evaluation occurs after selection. Required outputs include accuracy against ground
truth, macro-F1, a 12-class confusion matrix, per-class recall, reference-decision agreement,
point-margin distributions, and timing by declared scope. These outputs are recorded for
the completed feasibility run. Adjacent prediction stability is measured for the snapshot,
while ECDR/reference agreement is not established; certificate and full ECDR cost outputs
remain pending.


1.4   Research questions and hypotheses
RQ1 (reference quality) What classification accuracy and per-class recall does the new
  K=12 reference achieve against declared ground truth?
RQ2 (decision preservation) Does ECDR preserve the reference decision with zero dis-
  agreements on stable and transition windows under the specified certificate and lifecy-
  cle rules?
RQ3 (coverage and cost) At what event-conditioned coverage does ECDR reduce total end-
  to-end cost relative to evaluating the reference on every window, and how does that

  compare with a fixed fair-cost policy?
RQ4 (soundness) Which certificate bounds, membership predicates, and lifecycle obliga-

The hypothesis ledger is conditional. H1 predicts that stable windows will have more stable
adjacent reference predictions than transition windows; this is not ECDR/reference agree-
ment; the current snapshot measures it descriptively from 2,560/2,617 stable pairs versus
23/44 transition pairs. H2 predicts that event-conditioned certified coverage can improve at
a fixed matched total cost; this is proposed and requires a fair end-to-end benchmark. H3 is
a derived theorem obligation: if, for a candidate region Sr and predicted class c, every com-
peting margin satisfies infX∈Sr (Fc (X) − Fj (X)) > 0 for j ̸= c, then h(X) = c throughout that
region. This implication is mathematical, not a validated runtime result; executable bound,
arithmetic, membership, and lifecycle checks remain pending. H4 predicts net savings only
in an operating regime whose complete cost is measured.


1.5   Domain, event, and certificate semantics

An anchor is a specific window X r actually evaluated by the frozen reference, with trusted
class c = h(X r ). A candidate region is a set of admissible windows, a certificate is a sound
lower-bound statement over that region, and membership is the runtime predicate that de-
cides whether a new window belongs to it. These are separate objects. Event descriptors
are candidate signals for choosing when to rebuild or test a certificate; they do not prove
coverage by smoothness. The whole-domain condition above is a derived condition over
all 11 competing margins, not evidence that the condition holds for the current checkpoint.
Reference weights, BatchNorm running statistics, preprocessing, tie-breaking, and arith-
metic mode must remain fixed across a certificate’s lifecycle. Cached hits do not establish
a new trusted anchor: reuse is allowed only for a valid certificate and membership result,
otherwise the system falls back to the frozen reference.

The class IDs are fixed as follows: 1 WALKING, 2 WALKING_UPSTAIRS, 3 WALK-

9 SIT_TO_LIE, 10 LIE_TO_SIT, 11 STAND_TO_LIE, and 12 LIE_TO_STAND. The order is part of
the checkpoint identity.

2.2     Certificate lifecycle and architecture-specific firewall

Reuse is permitted only when a valid certificate exists and membership holds. Otherwise
the system falls back to the fixed reference evaluation. A hit does not renew the trusted
anchor. Model or preprocessing changes invalidate the certificate, as do recording or gap-
boundary changes that violate the declared region. The mathematical condition quantifies
every point in the candidate region and every one of the 11 competing classes; it does not
assume that a stable window is correct, and point margins are not regional bounds.
The exact overlap between adjacent stride-64 windows is 64 samples across six channels,
hence 384 shared coordinates. This is a reuse opportunity, not a zero-difference input: the
remaining coordinates change, and the full-input distance must be computed under the
declared norm if a bound requires it. The old valid-convolution/GAP feature cancellation
formulas are therefore firewalled from this BatchNorm–MaxPool–Flatten graph. Week 3
must derive or verify bounds on this actual graph.

Kết hợp hai chiều cho Θ(m) trong mô hình này. SIMD hoặc parallel hardware có thể thay
đổi wall-clock; theorem không cấm vectorization. Một conservative filter có thể false reject,
nhưng nếu luôn reject thì trivially an toàn mà không hữu ích. Bound tiệm cận không chứng
minh một checker nhanh hơn CNN nhỏ trong runtime cụ thể.
Với PCA, tính scores/residual theo basis dense có thể tốn O(dq); không phải cùng giá của
box. Với miền c0 + Bu + v dạng Minkowski sum tổng quát, membership là tìm tồn tại u, v
thỏa constraints. Một decomposition witness hợp lệ đủ để accept; projection bất kỳ thất bại
không chứng minh query ở ngoài miền, trừ khi đã chứng minh tính tương đương cho miền
đó.

16.1   One-step composition

Định lý 16.1 (Sound reuse gate). Giả sử reference identity khớp, lower margins hợp lệ và
dương trên S, membership chỉ accept khi query thật sự thuộc S. Policy trả c khi accept, còn
lại chạy reference, trả đúng reference decision trên mọi input hợp lệ trong phạm vi giả thiết.

Chứng minh. Nhánh accept dùng Định lý 9.1; nhánh fallback trả kết quả của chính refer-
ence. Hai nhánh bao hết trường hợp.

Policy trên không cho phép tự đổi center sau một hit. Ví dụ F1 (x) = 1 − x, F2 = 0 chứng nhận

cầu theo một bán kính cố định.


16.2   Fixed-anchor multi-step certificate

Định lý 16.2 (Nhiều query dùng cùng certificate). Giả sử toàn bộ các điều kiện của Định
lý 16.1 đúng, đặc biệt có certificate với lower margins hợp lệ và dương trên Sr . Giữ cố định
anchor, target c, identity và domain Sr . Với bất kỳ chuỗi hữu hạn hay vô hạn các query hợp lệ,
nếu mỗi lần reuse đều kiểm query hiện tại thuộc Sr bằng checker sound, mọi reused decision
đúng reference. Không yêu cầu query được reuse trước phải làm anchor mới.

Chứng minh. Tại mỗi thời điểm accept, áp dụng trực tiếp certificate trên Sr ; không phụ
thuộc các query trước. Tương đương quy nạp theo chiều dài prefix của trace. Khi miss,
policy chạy reference và chỉ xây certificate mới theo các điều kiện giống ban đầu.

Định lý đóng bài toán soundness của fixed-anchor policy. Nó không cho hit rate dương hoặc
bảo đảm anchor vẫn hữu ích mãi. Nếu certificate được xây riêng cho transition với overlap
cố định của window cũ, checker phải kiểm cả state/domain identity đó; không được chỉ
kiểm new wave rồi tự áp cho overlap khác sau một hit.

       state
